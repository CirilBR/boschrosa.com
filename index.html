Ciril Bosch-Rosa

Chair of Macroeconomics, Technische Universität Berlin

I am a behavioral economist at the Chair of Macroeconomics at TU Berlin. I specialize in surveys and experimental methods to study how people form beliefs, interact with each other, and ultimately make choices in complex environments.
